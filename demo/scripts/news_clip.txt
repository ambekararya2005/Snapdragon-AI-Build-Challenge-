# Kavach demo script: TV news report about a digital-arrest scam, third person only. FICTIONAL people and figures.
# About 45 seconds. Lines starting with '#' are comments and are not spoken.
Good evening. A retired teacher in Pune has lost forty lakh rupees in what police are calling a digital arrest scam.
According to the complaint, the fraudsters called the victim pretending to be CBI officers and claimed that a parcel in his name contained drugs.
The scammers kept him on a video call for two days and told him not to tell his family.
Police said the caller asked him to move his savings into a so called safe account for verification.
Cyber crime officials say no government agency conducts investigations over video calls.
The Reserve Bank has also warned that it never asks citizens to transfer money.
If you receive such a call, disconnect immediately and report it on the national cyber crime helpline, one nine three zero.
