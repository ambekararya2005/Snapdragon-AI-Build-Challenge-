'Digital arrest' scam: retired teacher loses Rs 40 lakh to fake CBI officers

Chronicle News Desk / Updated: Sep 27, 2026, 22:14 IST

PUNE: A 67-year-old retired teacher was duped of Rs 40 lakh by fraudsters who posed as CBI and customs officials and kept him under a so-called digital arrest for two days, police said.

According to police, the scammers told the victim that an arrest warrant had been issued in his name and that his bank account would be frozen. They threatened legal action unless he transferred money to a "safe account" for verification. They also told him not to tell anyone.

The cyber crime cell said such calls are fake. Real agencies never conduct a digital arrest over a video call.

How to stay safe: never share an OTP, never install AnyDesk on a stranger's request, and report at the national cyber crime helpline 1930.
