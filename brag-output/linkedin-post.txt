"This is the CBI. Don't disconnect the call. Install AnyDesk."

That's how most "digital arrest" scams begin. The scammer never touches your PC. They talk you into doing everything yourself: install a remote-access app, open net banking, read out the OTP.

For the Snapdragon AI Build & Present Challenge, I spent 2 days building Kavach (कवच, "armour") solo: an offline AI scam shield for Windows PCs.

It watches the 3 things every screen-sharing scam needs:
🖥️ a remote-access app running (AnyDesk, TeamViewer, Quick Assist and more)
🏦 a banking, UPI or OTP page on screen, read with on-device OCR
📞 scam tactics in the call (authority, threat, secrecy, urgency, money-move), transcribed on-device with Whisper

When they show up together, Kavach puts up a full-screen warning in Hindi and English, and one click freezes the scammer's remote session. I tested it with real AnyDesk controlled from my phone. The phone froze.

What I'm proud of:
✅ 10/10 scam scenarios caught, 0 false alarms across 10 normal ones (net banking, real IT support, news about scams)
✅ ~2 s median from the scam phrase to the warning
✅ All 6 models compiled for the Snapdragon Hexagon NPU with Qualcomm AI Hub. Speech recognition is ~6× faster than on a laptop CPU (117 ms vs 723 ms per 5 s of audio, AI Hub profile)
✅ Nothing leaves the PC: no network calls, and no screenshots, text or audio on disk

What I learned:
• Tuning weights couldn't tell a scammer from a genuine IT helper on AnyDesk. One rule could: no alert without an actual scam tactic.
• Test the scariest part first. The real AnyDesk test from my phone was the biggest risk in the whole demo.
• A small test set, honestly labelled, is worth more than one that looks perfect.

I didn't win, but I came out of it with a working product I'm proud of.
